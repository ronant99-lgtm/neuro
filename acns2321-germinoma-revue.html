Stratum I — Localized Germinoma, Complete Response

PRIMARY ENDPOINT · Target n per stratum not disclosed · Recruiting
Stratum Entry Criteria
Inclusion in Stratum I
Location
Localized midline germinoma — suprasellar and/or pineal. Includes bifocal (pineal + suprasellar). Contiguous ventricular / unifocal parenchymal extension acceptable.
Response
Complete Response (CR) after 4 cycles of induction chemotherapy with normalization of markers. Continued CR (CCR) on restaging MRI. PR with normalized markers eligible for second-look surgery → if mature teratoma or non-viable tumor → qualifies for Stratum I RT.
Markers
Normalized hCGβ and AFP at time of RT assignment.
Induction Chemotherapy (all strata)
Chemotherapy — Induction Phase
Regimen 4 Cycles · q21d
Carboplatin
IV over 15–60 min · Day 1 of each cycle
Etoposide
IV over 90–120 min · Days 1–3 of each cycle
Doses per COG institutional protocol. No ifosfamide — simpler regimen than ACNS1123 predecessors for NGGCT. Cycles repeat every 21 days × 4 cycles in absence of progression or unacceptable toxicity.
Response Assessment After Cycle 4
MRI brain + spine with gadolinium. Serum and CSF tumor markers (hCGβ, AFP). Restaging determines stratum assignment. Second-look surgery offered if PR or less with normalized markers.
Radiation Therapy — Stratum I
Radiothérapie — Timing · Volumes · Doses · Stratum I
Timing After Cycle 4 + Response Assessment PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
RT initiated after completion of 4 cycles of induction chemotherapy and confirmation of CR/CCR or eligibility via second-look surgery findings. Delivered once daily, 5 days/week.
Target Volumes
WVI Volume
Whole Ventricular Irradiation (WVI) — entire ventricular system. Lateral ventricles, third ventricle, fourth ventricle, cerebral aqueduct.
Based on subependymal relapse patterns seen in prior focal RT trials (SIOP-CNS-GCT-96, ACNS0232).
Boost Volume
Pre-treatment tumor volume (GTVpre-chemo) + margin per institutional convention → CTV/PTV. Includes original primary site(s).
For bifocal tumors: boost to both primary sites.
Technique
3D-CRT or IMRT. 5 fractions/week. QD delivery.
Imaging Guidance
Pre-RT MRI with gadolinium required. MRI-based planning preferred. Post-op MRI within 72h of surgery if resection performed.
Doses — Stratum I DOSE-REDUCED vs ACNS1123
Phase 1 — WVI
12 Gy 16 fractions · 0.75 Gy/fr · 5 fx/week · ~3.2 weeks
↓ vs ACNS1123 Stratum 2 (18 Gy WVI). Primary endpoint: non-inferiority of EFS at 2 and 3 years.
Phase 2 — Boost to Primary Site
12 Gy Sequential boost · Additional ~2.4 weeks
Cumulative dose at primary site: 24 Gy total. Boost volume = pre-treatment tumor. Same boost dose as ACNS1123.
⚠️ Fractionnement exact non publié (protocole opérationnel COG non public). Les 16 fr pour 12 Gy sont déduites d'une hypothèse 0.75 Gy/fr — à vérifier sur la version COG officielle.
Non-normalized markers / PD
Patients with non-normalized tumor markers OR progressive disease AND no second-look surgery (or viable tumor at second-look) → discontinued from protocol therapy. Do not receive Stratum I RT.

Strata IV & V — Metastatic Germinoma

Exploratory Endpoints · CSI-based RT · Recruiting
Stratum Entry — Metastatic Definition
Metastatic = Non-contiguous or Distant Disease
Stratum IV
Metastatic germinoma achieving CR after induction (± second-look surgery with mature teratoma / non-viable tumor). Markers normalized.
CSI 18 Gy
Stratum V
Metastatic germinoma with normalized markers failing to achieve CR. No second-look surgery performed.
CSI 24 Gy
Radiation Therapy — Strata IV & V
Radiotherapy — Craniospinal Irradiation · Metastatic Strata
Target Volume CSI — Entire Neuraxis
Craniospinal Volume
Full brain (whole brain) + entire spinal canal to thecal sac. Standard CSI field arrangement (posterior fossa matching with spinal fields or VMAT/IMRT CSI).
Boost Volumes
12 Gy boost to pre-treatment primary tumor volume AND all metastatic sites (non-contiguous disease / brain / ventricular / spinal lesions).
Doses
Stratum IV — CR after chemo
CSI 18 Gy + Boost 12 Gy
Cumulative at primary + mets: 30 Gy. Reduced CSI dose vs historical (24–30 Gy) for CR patients.
Stratum V — Less than CR
CSI 24 Gy + Boost 12 Gy
Cumulative at primary + mets: 36 Gy. Once daily, 5 days/week. 3DCRT or IMRT/VMAT.
Clinical Note Spinal Toxicity
CSI carries significant late effects risk in pediatric patients: growth hormone deficiency, thyroid dysfunction, hearing loss, ototoxicity, neurocognitive impact, vertebral growth effects. Proton CSI preferred when available for dose reduction to anterior structures. Endocrine monitoring mandatory throughout follow-up (every 3–4 months × 1 year, then per annual follow-up schedule).

Strata VI & VII — Basal Ganglia / Thalamic Germinoma

Exploratory Endpoints · WBI-based RT · Recruiting
Clinical Context — BG/Thalamic Germinomas
Basal ganglia and thalamic (BGTG) germinomas represent a diagnostically challenging subset — they often present with insidious motor or cognitive symptoms rather than classic endocrinopathy or visual field defects. Radiological mimics include gliomas, demyelination, and metabolic disease. Hemiatrophy ipsilateral to the tumor is a characteristic but not universal feature. Marker elevation (even mild hCGβ) in a unilateral hemispheric lesion should prompt germinoma workup. These tumors historically treated as "localized" disease but with unique radiation field considerations — WBI rather than WVI given parenchymal location outside ventricular system.
Stratum Entry
Stratum VI
Primary BG or thalamic germinoma — CR after chemo (± second-look).
WBI 18 Gy
Stratum VII
Primary BG or thalamic germinoma — less than CR (normalized markers, no surgery).
WBI 24 Gy
Radiation Therapy — Strata VI & VII
Radiotherapy — Whole Brain Irradiation · BG/Thalamic Strata

WBI Volume
Whole brain irradiation (not WVI) — entire brain parenchyma. Differs from WVI (ventricular only) given parenchymal tumor origin.
Boost Volume
Pre-treatment primary tumor volume (BG/thalamic lesion) → CTV/PTV boost. 12 Gy sequential boost.
Doses

WBI 18 Gy + Boost 12 Gy
Cumulative at tumor: 30 Gy. 3DCRT or IMRT.

Eligibility Criteria — ACNS2321

NCT06368817 · Ages 3–29 · All strata
Diagnostic Criteria — Markers & Histology
Germinoma Diagnosis — ACNS2321 Criteria
Category A — No biopsy required Classic presentation
Localized — Midline
Suprasellar and/or pineal lesion + hCGβ 5–50 mIU/mL (serum or CSF) + institutional normal AFP (or ≤10 ng/mL). Includes bifocal and contiguous extension.
Bifocal or Pineal + DI
Pineal + suprasellar OR pineal lesion + diabetes insipidus + hCGβ ≤100 mIU/mL + normal AFP.
Category B — Biopsy required Elevated hCGβ 51–100 mIU/mL
Histologic confirmation mandatory
hCGβ 51–100 mIU/mL (serum or CSF) + institutional normal AFP → histologic confirmation of germinoma required before enrollment.
Note: hCGβ-secreting GCT can cause false-positive pregnancy tests — confirm non-pregnant by institutional standard before enrollment.
Excluded histologic elements Ineligible
Endodermal sinus (yolk sac), embryonal carcinoma, choriocarcinoma, malignant/immature teratoma, mixed GCT with malignant elements → NGGCT protocol (ACNS2021, NCT04684368). Pure mature teratoma with negative markers → also excluded from ACNS2321.
Patient Eligibility
Inclusion
Age: ≥3 years and <30 years at enrollment
Newly diagnosed: No prior tumor-directed therapy (surgery and steroids allowed)
Performance: ECOG 0–1–2 (Karnofsky >16y; Lansky ≤16y)
Central imaging review: Eligibility confirmed by Rapid Central Imaging Review (APEC14B1-CNS)
Marker review: Eligibility confirmed by Rapid Central Tumor Marker Review
CSF: Lumbar CSF obtained prior to enrollment (unless contraindicated). Ventricular CSF at diversion acceptable for markers.
Spine MRI: With gadolinium at diagnosis — required
HIV: Eligible if on effective ART with undetectable viral load
Seizure disorder: Eligible if well-controlled on anticonvulsants
Exclusion
NGGCT elements: Yolk sac, embryonal Ca, choriocarcinoma, malignant teratoma
Pure mature teratoma with negative markers
Prior therapy: Any tumor-directed treatment (except surgery + steroids)
Pregnancy: Excluded — serum/urine test required (may be false positive due to hCGβ)
Breastfeeding
Status epilepticus, coma, or assisted ventilation
CNS toxicity >grade 2
Lab Requirements at Enrollment (within 7 days)
Organ Function Thresholds
Hematology
ANC ≥1000/μL · Platelets ≥100,000/μL (transfusion independent) · Hgb ≥8.0 g/dL
Hepatic
Total bilirubin ≤1.5× ULN for age · ALT ≤135 U/L (ULN set at 45 U/L for this study)
Renal (Pediatric)
Age-based serum creatinine thresholds OR CrCl ≥70 mL/min/1.73 m² OR GFR ≥50 mL/min/1.73 m² by direct measurement. eGFR not acceptable.
Renal (Adults ≥18y)
Cockcroft-Gault CrCl ≥70 mL/min or 24h urine collection.
Imaging Requirements
Brain MRI
With and without gadolinium at diagnosis / pre-enrollment. If surgical resection: pre-op AND post-op MRI (post-op within 72h of surgery). Biopsy only: post-op MRI recommended but not required. Must be within 31 days of enrollment.
Spine MRI
With gadolinium — required prior to enrollment. Perform before lumbar puncture (LP can obscure spinal MRI interpretation).
Timing
Enrollment and protocol therapy start: no later than 31 days after definitive surgery or clinical diagnosis, whichever is later.
CSF timing
Lumbar CSF ≥10 days post-surgery (false positive cytology can occur within 10 days). CSF markers + cytology required. Obtain after spine MRI.
Follow-up Schedule
Year 1
Every 3 months
Years 2–3
Every 4 months
Years 4–10
Annually
Neurocog
COG standardized battery at 9, 30, and 60 months post-diagnosis (processing speed, working memory, verbal learning, executive function, QoL)
Neuroendo
Serial endocrine function monitoring — GH, thyroid, gonadal axes. Cumulative incidence of neuroendocrine dysfunction is a key secondary endpoint.

COG Trial Lineage & Clinical Context

ACNS0232 → ACNS1123 → ACNS2321 · Evidence Base & Signal 2024–2026
Clinical Signal — 2024–2026
The central rationale for ACNS2321 is to test whether 12 Gy WVI (vs. 18 Gy in ACNS1123) maintains comparable disease control in complete responders, while potentially reducing late neurocognitive and neuroendocrine toxicity. The ACNS1123 CR result was striking: 3-year EFS ~94.4% in patients receiving 18 Gy WVI after carbo-etoposide induction. Prior ACNS0232 and SIOP-CNS-GCT-96 established that focal RT alone resulted in unacceptable ventricular relapse rates, driving the shift to WVI. The dose-reduction hypothesis rests on the assumption that the chemotherapy–RT interaction provides sufficient radiosensitization at 12 Gy to sterilize microscopic ventricular disease. Secondary endpoints targeting cognitive processing speed and neuroendocrine function reflect the field's recognition that survival-focused endpoints alone are insufficient in this largely curable, young population.
