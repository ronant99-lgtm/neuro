Clinical Signal 2025–2026

The paradigm of limited surgery + conformal RT (protons preferred) is now firmly established, with both SJCRH trials approaching final data in 2026. For papillary BRAF V600E+ craniopharyngiomas, integration of dabrafenib/trametinib before or after RT is the leading edge — potentially replacing or deferring RT in selected cases. Adamantinomatous tumors are now being targeted via MEK inhibition (binimetinib) and pan-RAF inhibition (tovorafenib).

The central open question: can targeted therapy defer or spare RT in biologically defined subgroups while preserving hypothalamic function long-term?

Clinical Signal 2025–2026

The LGG landscape is undergoing a paradigm shift: MAPK-targeted therapies are rapidly replacing chemotherapy as primary medical treatment and increasingly positioned to defer or spare RT. The LOGGIC/FIREFLY-2 Phase III trial (138 sites, 400 patients) comparing tovorafenib to standard chemotherapy in 1st-line is the most consequential trial currently open in pediatric neuro-oncology.

The Strasbourg trametinib trial and the Sainte-Justine mirdametinib trial signal important francophone network collaboration opportunities. RT remains standard for progressive/unresectable disease post-targeted therapy — its role is shifting to 2nd or 3rd line.

Clinical Signal 2025–2026

RT remains the indispensable platform — but the data confirm that dose and modality are not the primary outcome levers. The revolution lies in molecular stratification: H3 G34, H3 K27M, IDH, BRAF V600E now drive the choice of concurrent/adjuvant agent [1].

The COG AZD1390 trial (RT + ATM inhibitor, DDR radiosensitization) is the most structurally important new trial. LU-TARGET (radioligand post-RT) and CAR-T CD70 represent the most novel platforms entering Phase I.

Clinical Signal 2025–2026

Most rapidly evolving landscape of all five entities. RT 54 Gy remains the mandatory initial platform — now clearly positioned as the foundation for combination strategies rather than standalone treatment.

ONC201 (dordaviprone, Modeyso™): FDA accelerated approval August 2025 — first agent ever approved post-RT for DMG H3 K27M-mutant. Conditional on the ongoing Phase III ACTION trial [3].

CAR-T GD2 Phase I results (November 2024, n=11): median OS 20.6m, 4 major radiographic responses, 1 complete response >30 months — a paradigm-shifting signal. These two developments make DIPG/DMG the most-discussed topic expected at SJCRH observership 2026.

Clinical Signal 2025–2026

Medulloblastoma has the most mature molecular stratification framework directly integrated into RT decision-making. The central open question is how far CSI dose can be reduced in WNT-activated tumors — with the critical caveat that non-molecularly-selected dose reduction carries a 10% excess relapse risk [2].

SJMB13 and SJiMB21 (St. Jude) will be the central discussion points during the observership. On the immunotherapy front, GPC2-CAR T (Stanford, recruiting) and B7-H3 CAR-T (Stanford/MSK, opening July 2026) represent the next cellular therapy wave — extending the DIPG CAR-T logic to embryonal tumor biology. MEPENDAX (AP-HM Marseille) is the principal French network opportunity for relapsed disease.
