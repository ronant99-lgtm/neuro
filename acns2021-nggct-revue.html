Plan A — WVSCI for Good Responders

CR or PR (± SLS) · Novel Volume: WVI + Spinal Canal · PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
Plan A Entry Criteria
Eligibility for Plan A (WVSCI)
Response
CR or PR after 6 cycles of induction chemotherapy with marker normalization (AFP + hCGβ). Includes patients with PR after second-look surgery showing mature teratoma or non-viable tumor only.
Markers
AFP and hCGβ normalized at time of RT assignment.
Timing
Within 6 weeks of the end of induction chemotherapy or second-look surgery.
Radiation Therapy — Plan A (WVSCI)
Radiotherapy — Novel WVSCI Volume · Timing · Doses
Target Volume WVSCI — Novel Volume
Whole Ventricular Irradiation (WVI)
Full ventricular system — lateral ventricles, third ventricle, cerebral aqueduct, fourth ventricle. Same volume as ACNS1123.
WVI replaces WBRT to reduce neurocognitive late effects — no ventricular relapses were seen on ACNS1123.
Spinal Canal Irradiation
Entire spinal canal from foramen magnum to thecal sac. Added to WVI to address the 12% isolated spinal relapse rate seen on ACNS1123 with WVI-only.
Modern techniques (IMRT/VMAT/proton) minimize dose to tissues anterior to the spinal canal (vertebral bodies, abdominal organs).
Tumor Bed Boost Volume
Pre-treatment primary tumor volume (GTV pre-chemo) + margin per institutional convention → CTV/PTV boost. Includes original primary site.
Technique
3D-CRT or IMRT/VMAT. Proton therapy eligible. QD delivery, 5 fractions/week. Online contouring atlas provided by COG.
Doses — Plan A REDUCED vs ACNS0122
Phase 1 — WVSCI (WVI + Spinal Canal)
30.6 Gy 17 fractions · 1.8 Gy/fr · 5 fx/week · ~3.4 weeks
Same dose as ACNS1123 WVI, now extended to include spinal canal. Reduced vs ACNS0122 CSI (36 Gy). No ventricular relapses on ACNS1123 → 30.6 Gy adequate for microscopic ventricular disease.
Phase 2 — Tumor Bed Boost
54 Gy total 13 additional fractions · 1.8 Gy/fr · sequential boost
Boost volume = pre-treatment tumor. Cumulative dose at primary: 54 Gy. Total treatment ~6 weeks (17 fr + 13 fr = 30 fractions).
Key Concept WVSCI vs CSI
WVSCI is a novel radiation volume — it combines ventricular irradiation (brain component) with spinal canal irradiation (spinal component), but critically avoids whole-brain RT. This means cortical brain parenchyma outside the ventricular system is spared, unlike traditional CSI where the entire brain receives the full prescription dose. The goal: retain spinal disease control (lost on ACNS1123) while minimizing the neurocognitive burden of WBRT (as used in ACNS0122).
Proton vs Photon Secondary Objective
Prospective comparison of proton vs photon outcomes (cognitive, auditory, neuroendocrine, spinal growth) is a key secondary objective. For patients <13 years, vertebral body inclusion/exclusion is specifically evaluated. Proton therapy preferred when available for anterior dose reduction.

Second-look surgery (SLS) is central to ACNS2021 response assessment. Unlike germinomas (ACNS2321), NGGCT frequently contains mature teratoma that does not respond to chemotherapy but is biologically benign — SLS can reclassify apparent poor responders to Plan A eligibility. Barrier documentation and clinical benefit assessment of SLS is a specific secondary objective of this trial. AFP normalization timing is critical: elevated AFP >10 ng/mL after induction raises concern for persistent viable malignant elements.

Treatment repeats every 21 days for up to 6 cycles in the absence of disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. CarboE alternates with IE. Total induction duration ~18 weeks. G-CSF (filgrastim or pegfilgrastim) per institutional standards.

MRI brain + spine with gadolinium. Serum and CSF tumor markers (AFP, hCGβ). Response determines Plan assignment (A vs B). Second-look surgery offered per decision algorithm.

Eligibility Criteria — ACNS2021

NCT04684368 · Ages 3–29 · Localized NGGCT · Suprasellar/Pineal
Diagnostic Criteria — Markers & Histology
NGGCT Diagnosis — ACNS2021 Criteria
Eligible Histologies NGGCT elements
Endodermal sinus tumor (yolk sac), embryonal carcinoma, choriocarcinoma, malignant/immature teratoma, and mixed GCT (may include germinoma component if malignant NGGCT elements are present).
Marker-Based Diagnosis Without biopsy
AFP elevated above institutional normal OR >10 ng/mL, OR hCGβ >100 mIU/mL (serum or CSF) in a suprasellar/pineal lesion. Confirmed by Rapid Central Marker Screening Review (APEC14B1-CNS).
Histologic confirmation not mandatory if markers are diagnostic.
Excluded Ineligible
Pure mature teratoma with non-elevated markers. Basal ganglia or other non-ventricular primary sites. Metastatic disease (MRI, CSF cytology, or intraoperative evidence of dissemination). Pure germinoma → ACNS2321 (NCT06368817).
Patient Eligibility
Inclusion
Age: ≥3 years and <30 years at enrollment
Location: Suprasellar and/or pineal region. Bifocal tumors included.
Newly diagnosed: No prior tumor-directed therapy (surgery + steroids allowed)
Central review: Eligibility confirmed by Rapid Central Imaging Review + Rapid Central Marker Review
CSF: Lumbar CSF obtained prior to enrollment (unless contraindicated). Within 31 days of enrollment.
Spine MRI: With gadolinium at diagnosis — required. Obtain before LP.
Serum markers: AFP + hCGβ within 7 days of enrollment
CSF markers: Within 31 days of enrollment; ≥10 days post-surgery
Exclusion
Basal ganglia / thalamic or other non-ventricular primary sites
Metastatic disease at diagnosis
Pure mature teratoma with non-elevated markers
Prior therapy: Any tumor-directed treatment (except surgery + steroids)
Pregnancy: Excluded (false positive possible due to hCGβ)
Breastfeeding
Sexually active without effective contraception
Neurocognitive Assessment
Language
English, Spanish, or French-speaking (other languages participate but without neurocog assessment)
Battery
COG Standardized Neuropsychological Battery + BRIEF-2, PedsQL, ABAS-3, BASC-3 questionnaires
Exclusion
Pre-existing neurodevelopmental disorder (Down syndrome, Fragile X, intellectual disability). NF1 allowed.

COG NGGCT Trial Lineage & Clinical Context

ACNS0122 → ACNS1123 → ACNS2021 · Evidence Base & Signal 2024–2026
Clinical Signal — 2024–2026
The defining lesson from ACNS1123 NGGCT Stratum 1 is unambiguous: eliminating spinal irradiation led to 12% isolated spinal relapse rate (6 of 66 evaluable patients), prompting early study closure of the NGGCT arm. This is despite encouraging 3-year PFS of 87.8% — the pattern of failure was unacceptable given the near-zero salvage rates for relapsed NGGCT (5-year OS 9–14% in the EU-RHAB registry).

ACNS2021 represents a carefully calibrated course correction: it reintroduces spinal irradiation via the novel WVSCI volume while preserving the neurocognitive advantage of WVI over whole-brain RT. The 30.6 Gy dose for microscopic disease was validated by the absence of ventricular failures on ACNS1123, and is maintained in ACNS2021 rather than returning to 36 Gy CSI.

For poor responders, Plan B introduces frontline HDCSCR (etoposide/thiotepa + PBSCT) — investigational in the newly diagnosed setting but supported by salvage data showing that standard-dose re-treatment has essentially zero efficacy in relapsed NGGCT.
